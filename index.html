Our Purpose

Why the Kindness Board Matters

In healthcare and palliative care, medical treatment is only one half of healing. Emotional warmth, spiritual comfort, and knowing that someone out there is caring for you can transform a patient’s journey.

Organized by our College Social Welfare Club in partnership with regional Palliative Care Centres, the Kindness Board collects heartfelt messages from our academic community and gently delivers them directly to patients, caregivers, and hospice staff.

🌿

Spreading Hope

Reminding patients that they are valued, remembered, and surrounded by warm thoughts.

❤️

Honoring Caregivers

Expressing deep gratitude to nurses, doctors, and family members who selflessly care for others.

🛡️

Safe & Moderated

Every submission is reviewed by student moderators to maintain a safe, uplifting environment.

Initiative Journey

Event Timeline

From initial message collection to physical delivery at the palliative care center.

Phase 1 • Launch

Campus Campaign Kickoff

Kindness Board digital platform launched across college departments and student portals.

Phase 2 • Ongoing

Message Collection

Students, faculty, and campus visitors write anonymous and signed words of encouragement.

Phase 3 • Moderation

Review & Curation

Student welfare team approves submissions and curates printable message booklets.

Phase 4 • Delivery

Care Centre Visit

Volunteers visit the Palliative Care Unit to deliver printed Kindness Booklets & digital displays.

Phase 5 • Completion

Impact & Certificates

Distribution of digital participation certificates to all registered contributors.
