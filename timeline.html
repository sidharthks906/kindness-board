Initiative Journey

Event Timeline

From initial message collection across college departments to physical delivery at partner palliative care centers.

Phase 1 • Launch

Campus Campaign Kickoff

Kindness Board digital platform launched across college departments, social welfare clubs, and student portals.

Phase 2 • Ongoing

Message Collection

Students, faculty, and campus visitors write anonymous and signed words of encouragement, prayers, and caregiver thanks.

Phase 3 • Moderation

Review & Curation

Student welfare team approves submissions, scans for profanity, and curates printable message booklets.

Phase 4 • Delivery

Care Centre Visit

Volunteers visit the Palliative Care Unit to deliver printed Kindness Booklets & digital displays to patients and nurses.

Phase 5 • Completion

Impact & Certificates

Distribution of digital participation certificates to all registered student and faculty contributors.

Be Part of Phase 2 Today

Message collection is currently active. Add your message to the physical booklet before delivery!
